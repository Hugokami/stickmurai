Combat Guide & Techniques

  • Movement: Use WASD / Arrow Keys on Desktop, or the Virtual Joystick on Mobile devices.
  • Slash (Basic Attack): Left-click / LMB on Desktop or tap SLASH on Mobile. Auto-aims toward targets.
  • Dodge / Dash: Press SPACEBAR on Desktop or tap DASH on Mobile. Full invulnerability frames.
  • Perfect Dodge: Dash right as an enemy lunges to trigger a screen flash, a combo boost, and 2s of invulnerability.
  • Parrying: Hold SLASH to charge, and release it at the exact moment of an incoming attack to Parry.
  • Iaijutsu Shockwave: Hold SLASH and release at full charge to unleash a piercing crescent projectile.
  • Flow Awakening (Ultimate): Defeat enemies to fill the FLOW meter. Press F Key or ULT to enter time-slowed state.
  • Progression & Leveling: Gain EXP to level up and choose powerful character upgrades.
  • Difficulty Modes: Select your challenge before starting.
  • Auto-Aim: Basic attacks automatically target the closest enemy within 600px range.
  • Iaijutsu PvP: Enter the PvP Lobby, copy your unique Ronin UID, add friends by their UID, and invite them directly to a 1v1 duel. No code sharing required!
  • Combo - Swift Counter: Dash + Slash immediately. Teleports behind the target for a quick slash.
  • Combo - Thunderclap & Flash: Dash + Slash during Storm God or with Raijin Step. Slashes through targets with lightning.
  • Combo - Rising Dragon: Slash + Slash + release charge early. Launches enemies into the air.
  • Combo - Mirror Strike: Fully charged Iaijutsu Slash while invisible (from Decoy active passive). Teleports clones to strike.
