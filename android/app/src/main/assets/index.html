Combat Guide & Techniques

  • Movement: Use WASD / Arrow Keys on Desktop, or the Virtual Joystick on Mobile devices to move around the battlefield.
  • Slash (Basic Attack): Left-click / LMB on Desktop or tap SLASH on Mobile. Auto-aims toward nearest targets within 600px. Chain hits to build Combo!
  • Dodge / Dash: Press SPACE on Desktop or tap DASH on Mobile. Full invulnerability frames (i-frames).
  • Perfect Dodge: Dash right as an enemy lunges with a red alert to trigger a screen flash, a massive Combo boost, and 2s of total invulnerability.
  • Parrying & Deflection: Hold LMB to charge, and release it at the exact moment of impact. Parrying consecutively raises musical pitch and upgrades deflection sparks from Amber ➔ Gold ➔ Cyan ➔ White-Blue!
  • Posture Break & Execution: Heavy hits and deflections fill the enemy's Posture meter. When full, strike to trigger a cinematic Execution dealing lethal damage and stance synergy detonations!
  • Chiburui & Noto (血振るい): Stand completely still for 1.2s after 3+ kills. Cleanses blade, grants +15 Flow, and your next strike deals guaranteed 2.5x CRIT damage!
  • Iaijutsu Shockwave: Hold LMB and release at full charge to unleash a piercing crescent shockwave.
  • Flow Awakening (Ultimate): Defeat enemies to fill the FLOW meter. Press F Key or tap ULT on Mobile to unleash an awakened ultimate.
  • Combo - Swift Counter: DASH + SLASH immediately. Teleports behind the target for a quick slash.
  • Combo - Thunderclap & Flash: DASH + SLASH during Storm God or Raijin Step. Teleport-cleaves through targets with lightning.
  • Combo - Rising Dragon: SLASH × 2 + release charge early. Launches enemies into an airborne whirlwind.
  • Combo - Mirror Strike: Fully charged Iaijutsu Slash while invisible from Decoy. Teleports 3 clones to strike simultaneously!
